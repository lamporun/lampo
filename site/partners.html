How it works

  1. Enter your Lightning address and get your link: https://lampo.run/r/<code>/v1.
  2. Use it wherever an OpenAI base URL goes: in your agent, your integration, your tutorial, your OpenClaw config or your MCP server's default.
  3. Each bundle bought through it credits you 10% of its price: 100 sats for a 1,000-sat bundle, 300 for a 3,000-sat one.
  4. Once you're owed 1,000 sats or more, we send them to your Lightning address automatically, within minutes. We pay the routing fee.

Partner terms

  • Commission. 10% of the price of every bundle paid through your link, with no end date. Only paid bundles count. Lampo's own test purchases never count.
  • Payouts. Sent to your Lightning address once you're owed at least 1,000 sats, the smallest payment our wallet can send. If a payout fails, we try again a few hours later. If its outcome is unclear, we check it by hand before paying anything else.
  • Your own link. You may buy through your own link. It works as a 10% discount.
  • Fair play. No spam. No misleading claims about Lampo or its models. Don't present yourself as Lampo, and don't run paid ads on the Lampo name. We may suspend a link that breaks these rules. Commission already earned through legitimate purchases is still paid.
  • Changes. We may change these terms or end the program with 30 days' notice on this page. Commission earned until then is paid.
  • Taxes. You are responsible for any taxes on your earnings.
  • Privacy. We store your Lightning address to pay you, and show it masked on your dashboard. Requests through partner links are logged without IP addresses or headers, only so we can match purchases to your link. See the privacy notice.
