nwc-guard: a spending firewall for your agent's wallet

Agents are getting Lightning wallets. One poisoned web page, email or tool result can tell an agent to pay an attacker. nwc-guard sits between the agent and your wallet, and nothing new gets paid without your ✅.

Hosted beta: coming soon. Nothing to install or keep online. Your agent gets a guarded connection, and you approve payments from Telegram.

What it checks, on every payment

  • Who gets paid. The payee is recovered from the invoice signature, and a payee you haven't approved waits for you.
  • How much. A cap per payment, plus hourly and daily budgets that count fees and payments still in flight.
  • How often. At most N payments a minute, so a drain made of many small payments stops early.
  • Never twice. The same invoice is never paid again, even after a wallet timeout.
  • Only what's needed. The agent sees its own payments and what it may still spend today. Keysend, signing and hold invoices are blocked.
  • A stop button. /stop on Telegram pauses everything.

Run it yourself today

The open-source version (MIT) runs on any machine with Node.js:

npx github:lamporun/nwc-guard init --name my-agent
NWC_UPSTREAM_URL="nostr+walletconnect://..." npx github:lamporun/nwc-guard start

Setup, rules and Telegram approvals: github.com/lamporun/nwc-guard. It works with any NWC wallet and any NWC app: MCP servers, OpenClaw skills, l402-bridge.

Tested on mainnet

On 24 November 2026 nwc-guard sat between l402-bridge and a Minibits wallet, buying AI inference from Lampo. The first bundle waited for approval on Telegram and was paid after ✅. A bundle over the limit was refused without asking, and nothing was paid.
