Privacy

Version of 22 November 2026 · information under Art. 13 GDPR

In short

No accounts, no cookies, no tracking. We do not store your prompts or the model's responses.

Controller

Unnecessary Pixels, Cesano Maderno (MB), Italy · contact@lampo.run.

What we process

  • Requests. Your request passes through our server to the model provider to generate the response, and the response streams back to you. We keep neither (performance of the contract, Art. 6(1)(b)). The upstream provider processes them only to serve the request.
  • Payments and usage. For each bundle we keep an anonymous credential identifier, the model, the price in satoshis, the tokens used and the time. They contain no name, account or email. We keep them for 10 years for accounting and tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • IP addresses. Used in memory to limit abuse and to block access from countries under EU sanctions, by comparing them with public lists of IP ranges (legitimate interest and legal obligation, Art. 6(1)(f) and (c)). Our web server writes no general access log, and the payment gateway does not log IP addresses for normal requests; a rare error entry may include one. All server logs are deleted after 14 days.
  • Partner links. Requests made through a partner link (/r/<code>/v1) are logged without IP addresses or request headers: only the time, the path, the response status and the payment challenge, so a purchase can be credited to the partner (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). These logs rotate and stay small.
  • nwc-guard waitlist. If you join the hosted nwc-guard beta through @LampoGuard_bot, we keep your Telegram chat id, username and first name to write to you about the beta (consent, Art. 6(1)(a)). Send /leave to the bot to be removed at any time.
  • Partners. If you join the partner program, we store your Lightning address and your commissions and payouts, to pay you and for accounting (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)). Your dashboard shows the address masked.

Who else is involved

The server runs in Italy (Aruba, Arezzo). Model inference is provided by Baseten in the United States, as our data processor under its data processing agreement; for requests served in real time Baseten states that it does not store inputs or outputs.

The DeepSeek, Kimi (Moonshot AI), GLM (Z.ai) and Nemotron (NVIDIA) models are open-weight models run by Baseten in the United States. No data is sent to the companies that developed them or to China.

Bitcoin Lightning payments are handled by our self-custodial Wavelength wallet and the Wavelength swap service operated by Lightning Labs.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, or object to processing, by writing to the contact above. Since we hold no identifying data, we may be unable to link a request to you. You can complain to the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it) or the one in your country.
